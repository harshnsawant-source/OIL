Meridian Consulting — New Client Onboarding Checklist

1. Signed Master Services Agreement on file.
2. Signed mutual NDA BEFORE any client data is shared or accessed.
3. Client data is processed on-premises only; it must never be uploaded to
   third-party cloud AI services. This is a hard requirement of our engagements.
4. Provision a dedicated, access-controlled project workspace.
5. Identify the client's single point of contact and an escalation contact.
6. Schedule the kickoff meeting within 10 business days of contract signature.
7. Confirm invoicing details and billing contact.

Security note: access to client documents is logged. Any access to confidential
material is recorded with a timestamp and the document accessed.
