# Master Services Agreement

**Parties:** Meridian Consulting LLC ("Provider") and Acme Corporation ("Client").
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025.
**Term:** This Agreement remains in effect for twelve (12) months from the
Effective Date and renews automatically for successive 12-month terms unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before
the end of the then-current term.

## 1. Fees
The Client shall pay Provider a monthly retainer of **$12,000 USD**. Fees are
invoiced on the 15th of each month. Payment is due **net 30** from the invoice
date. Overdue amounts accrue a late charge of **1.5% per month**.

## 2. Scope
Provider will deliver data-platform engineering and advisory services as
described in each Statement of Work (SOW). No SOW is effective until signed by
both parties.

## 3. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information and will not
disclose it to third parties. Confidentiality obligations survive termination
for a period of **three (3) years**.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within
**thirty (30) days** of written notice. Upon termination, Client shall pay for
all services rendered through the termination date.

## 5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Delaware**.
