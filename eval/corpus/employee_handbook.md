# Meridian Consulting — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year.
Unused PTO of up to **5 days** may be carried over into the next year; any excess
is forfeited on December 31.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Mondays are designated
in-office days for all staff to support team collaboration.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses are reimbursed when accompanied by a receipt. **Receipts are
required for any expense over $50.** Reimbursement requests must be submitted
within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Personal expenses are not
reimbursable.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop to each employee. Employees are responsible for
keeping company equipment secure and must report loss or theft within 24 hours.
